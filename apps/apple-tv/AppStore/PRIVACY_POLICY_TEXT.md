LeliBrambas+ for Apple TV does not provide accounts, login, device activation, analytics, advertising, tracking, or user uploads. At startup the application requests the public movie catalogue from Cloudflare Workers, and it loads public poster artwork from Cloudflare R2. A catalogue copy and generic poster are included as fallbacks. When a viewer starts a preview or movie, Apple’s AVPlayer connects to the configured Cloudflare Stream video address to deliver that video.

The application does not intentionally collect an email address, user identifier, device activation code, session token, viewing history, or advertising identifier. Standard network information may be processed by Apple, the App Store, internet providers, and the video delivery provider under their respective terms and policies.
