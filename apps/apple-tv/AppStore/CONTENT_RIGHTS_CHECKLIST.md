# Content rights checklist

App Store distribution and streaming authorization are separate from technical access control. The owner must confirm rights before showing any item to testers or App Review.

For every catalog item and visual/audio asset, record outside source control:

- rights holder and evidence of ownership/license;
- permission to reproduce, stream, and distribute through a tvOS app;
- territories, audience, term, and any device/platform limits;
- performer/music/composition/recording releases where applicable;
- poster, backdrop, logo, font, studio-ident, and top-shelf artwork rights;
- whether App Review may access the item;
- takedown/contact process and expiry reminders.

Release gates:

- [ ] All submitted screenshots use synthetic or explicitly cleared imagery and disclose no private people/data without consent.
- [ ] The dedicated reviewer catalog contains enough cleared content to exercise playback.
- [ ] Every production title is authorized for the selected App Store territories and unlisted distribution model.
- [ ] Media hosting/CDN terms permit this client and signed-URL behavior.
- [ ] App Store Connect’s content-rights question is answered by the legal rights holder.
- [ ] A removal process exists for withdrawn/expired permission.

Do not infer distribution rights from possession of a file, family relationship, private access, or an unlisted App Store link.
