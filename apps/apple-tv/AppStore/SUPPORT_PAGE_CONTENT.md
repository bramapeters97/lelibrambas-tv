# LeliBrambas+ Apple TV Support

LeliBrambas+ is an invitation-only family film archive for Apple TV.

## Activate Apple TV

1. Open LeliBrambas+ and choose **Activate Apple TV**.
2. On a phone or computer, open the HTTPS address shown on the television or scan its QR code.
3. Sign in with an approved account, enter/confirm the displayed code, and approve the Apple TV.
4. Return to the television. Expired codes cannot be reused; request a new code.

Never send an activation code or password to anyone claiming to be support.

## Playback help

- Confirm Apple TV has internet access and the archive service is available.
- Retry from the details screen; signed playback links can expire.
- Use the Siri Remote for play/pause, scrubbing, and ten-second seeking.
- If one film fails while others play, report the title privately without sending its playback URL.
